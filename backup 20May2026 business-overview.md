# Business Overview

## The problem we solve

Small business owners know they need a website but every path is broken. Agencies are expensive, slow, and require a pile of information before they'll even start. DIY consumes hours and produces something mediocre. Subscription tools (Squarespace, Wix) feel templated and never quite fit. And once a site exists, it's never clear what's included in hosting and what costs extra, so owners often just stop touching it.

Our customer is the person who has felt all of this and decided either to skip having a website entirely or to spend nights and weekends cobbling one together. We are aiming squarely at that decision point.

## The model

Three things make this business work:

**Cold outreach with finished mockups, not pitches.** We send prospects 2–3 fully-rendered mockup websites of their business before asking for anything. This converts dramatically better than a discovery call request because it removes the leap of faith. Cost to us is compute plus a few minutes of human review.

**Lean operations via Claude-assisted creation.** The traditional cost structure of a web shop is people-hours. Ours shifts most of that to automation, with humans in the loop only for review and the genuinely high-touch parts (closing conversations, brand judgment calls, customer relationships).

**Hosting + light maintenance as recurring revenue.** Build fees alone produce lumpy income. The real business is monthly hosting with included small changes, priced transparently. Sticky because the customer doesn't want to migrate, but explicitly not locked-in because we'll export their site at any time (this is a feature — see `hosting-strategy.md`).

## What we are not

- Not an agency. No 20-page proposals, no 6-month projects, no "we'll get back to you next quarter."
- Not a SaaS tool. We don't make customers learn an editor; they email us changes.
- Not WordPress jockeys. Our sites are static and fast.
- Not a marketing agency. We make the website; we don't run their ads.

## Target customer

Single-location service businesses and small retailers, ideally with:
- A dated or broken existing website, or no website at all
- Budget for ~$200–500 upfront plus $25–100/mo ongoing
- Located in our outreach geography

Good initial verticals to test (pick one to start):
- Restaurants, cafés, bakeries
- Salons, barbers, spas
- Independent dentists, optometrists, chiropractors
- Solo or small-firm lawyers
- Contractors (HVAC, plumbing, electrical)
- Fitness studios, dance studios, martial arts
- Specialty retail (bookstores, boutiques, pet shops)

Bad fits (skip): large chains, multi-location businesses with corporate IT, anything regulated enough to need legal review on copy (medical, financial advisory), anything we couldn't comfortably show off in our portfolio.

## Principles

- Every customer should feel like the site was made *for them*, not adapted from a template.
- Updates should be fast and predictable. We don't disappear for two weeks to change a phone number.
- No platform lock-in, ever. The site is theirs. We'll hand it over on request.
- Honest pricing, published on our own site. The biggest betrayal in the agency world is hidden monthly creep.
- Quality over volume. Better to send 50 great mockups a week than 500 mediocre ones.

## Success metrics (early)

For the first 90 days, optimize for learning, not revenue:
- Cost per mockup batch (target: under $5 in API + tooling)
- Reply rate on cold outreach (baseline guess: 1–3%)
- Reply-to-call rate
- Call-to-close rate
- How many "this feels like a previous one" rejections during human review

Once a stable funnel exists, switch to: net new MRR per week, churn rate, support time per customer per month.
