Welcome Back to La Cucina

After years away, La Cucina has returned to the heart of Hanover stronger than ever. Owner Armando Malvone personally oversees every detail — from the thoughtfully designed dining room to the house-made specialties that emerge from our kitchen.

You'll find Armando greeting guests, sharing stories, and ensuring each visit feels special. This is Italian hospitality the way it should be: genuine, generous, and unforgettable.

Whether you're settling in at our lively bar or enjoying an intimate dinner, you're not just dining with us. You're becoming part of the La Cucina family.

From Our Kitchen

Every dish reflects our commitment to authentic Italian flavors and the finest ingredients. Our chefs craft each plate with care, creating meals that feel both familiar and extraordinary.

Our team is expertly trained in gluten-free and dietary accommodations. Just let us know how we can make your meal perfect for you.

Antipasto Excellence

Our signature antipasto platter showcases the finest Italian cured meats, artisan cheeses, and seasonal accompaniments. It's become legendary among our guests for good reason.

Fresh Pasta Daily

House-made pasta prepared fresh every day, served with traditional sauces that have been perfected over generations. Each bite transports you straight to Italy.

Signature Entrees

From tender osso buco to perfectly grilled branzino, our entrees celebrate the diversity and richness of Italian cuisine with a contemporary touch.

Our Bar

Step into our welcoming bar area for expertly crafted cocktails, an extensive Italian wine selection, and the kind of conversation that makes evenings memorable.

Walk-ins are always welcome at the bar, even on our busiest Saturday nights. Armando often stops by with a complimentary taste or special recommendation that turns a good night into a great one.

Whether you're here for aperitivo or staying for dinner, our bar captures the convivial spirit that makes Italian dining so special.
