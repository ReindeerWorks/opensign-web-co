About Little Fox

Our Story

We opened because Hanover needed a place where coffee and books could coexist peacefully. Not a café with a few novels on display, but a real bookshop that happens to brew exceptional drinks.

Every morning, we arrange our books like old friends waiting to meet new people. Every cup we serve is chosen for coffee lovers, not just caffeine seekers.

The Loft Experience

Climb our stairs to find our hidden loft reading area. It's the spot regular customers protect like a secret—quiet, tucked away, perfect for getting lost in your latest find.

Our staff loves talking books as much as coffee. Ask for recommendations. We read what we sell and drink what we serve.

Find Us in Downtown Hanover

We're right on Broadway, with paid street parking and wheelchair-accessible spots nearby. Come as you are—we accept all payment types except cash.
