The Warehouse Story

We started with a simple idea: take the bones of an old warehouse and fill it with warmth. Keep the industrial character but add house-made comfort food and fresh-brewed beer.

The result is a place where you can taste the care in every dish. Where our servers like Ethan and Marie know the menu inside out and help you find exactly what you're craving. Where you come for the globally-inspired food but stay for the atmosphere.

We're not trying to be fancy. We're trying to be the place you think of when the weather turns cold and you need somewhere warm, inviting, and real.
