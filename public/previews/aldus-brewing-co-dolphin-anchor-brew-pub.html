How Food Works Here

We focus on brewing great beer. For food, we partner with Hotel Hanover next door. It's simple once you know how.

1

Scan the QR code at your table

Every table has a code that connects you to Hotel Hanover's kitchen menu.

2

Order what looks good

The tuna wontons are excellent. Skip the loaded tots unless you're really hungry.

3

Food comes to you

Hotel Hanover delivers right to your table. You stay put, keep drinking, keep playing.

Why We're Different

Most brewpubs are loud. Ours isn't. We keep the volume low, put smooth jazz on the speakers, and give you space to actually talk to whoever you came with.

🎲

Games at Every Table

Puzzles, cards, and booth games. Because sometimes the best conversations happen over something to do with your hands.

🎵

Jazz, Not Sports

We'll change the TV if you ask, but we're not a sports bar. Think neighborhood living room with better beer.

🍺

Staff Who Actually Care

Our team gives real beer recommendations and seems genuinely pleased to help. It makes a difference.
