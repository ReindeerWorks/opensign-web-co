Why We're Here

Most brewpubs are loud. We're not. Most taprooms rush you through. We don't. We opened Dolphin & Anchor because Hanover's Center Square deserved a place where you could actually hear each other talk.

You'll find booth games, table puzzles, and staff who remember what you drank last time. The jazz stays smooth, the volume stays low, and the beer stays fresh. We're not trying to be everything to everyone. We're trying to be exactly what you need when you want to slow down.

Our brewing happens right here. Every beer starts in our system and ends in your glass. No shortcuts, no compromises. Just the kind of thoughtful brewing that pairs well with thoughtful conversation.
