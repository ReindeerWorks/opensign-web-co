Our Story

We started Zodiac Donuts because we believed everyone deserves amazing baked goods, regardless of dietary restrictions.

Too many people with allergies or dietary choices have to settle for "good enough" desserts. We spent months perfecting our recipes until our vegan and gluten-free options tasted just as indulgent as traditional versions.

Every batch is made fresh in small quantities. We rotate seasonal flavors to keep things exciting and source quality ingredients because shortcuts show up in the final product.

Our virtual bakery model lets us focus entirely on the baking while keeping costs reasonable for our customers.
