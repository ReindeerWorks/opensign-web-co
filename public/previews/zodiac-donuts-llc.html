About Zodiac Donuts

We started Zodiac Donuts because we believe dietary restrictions shouldn't mean settling for less. Every recipe is crafted to prove that vegan and gluten-free can taste better than conventional baking.

Our small-batch approach means we can experiment with seasonal flavors like lavender vanilla cream and blueberry lemon fritters. We're not just making donuts — we're making sure everyone has a seat at the dessert table.
